    1. What is the best app monetization strategy?

    The best strategy depends on the app type. Subscriptions work well for SaaS and AI apps, while ads work better for entertainment platforms.

    2. How do free apps make money?

    Free apps commonly generate revenue through:

  • ads
  • subscriptions
  • in-app purchases
  • affiliate systems

    • 3. Are subscriptions better than ads?

    For many apps, yes. Subscriptions often create more stable recurring revenue.

    4. Can AI apps make money easily?

    AI apps have strong monetization potential through:

  • subscriptions
  • AI credits
  • premium tools
  • automation services

    • 5. What is the biggest monetization mistake?

    Overloading apps with ads or charging users before delivering value.

## 5 Brand Building Mistakes That Cost Small Businesses Clients

**Starting with a logo instead of a strategy**
A logo without a strategy is decoration. Brand building starts with understanding your audience and positioning, not your colour palette.

**Using separate vendors for each piece**
When a designer, developer, and SEO consultant all work in isolation, nothing connects. Inconsistency erodes trust.

**Treating SEO as optional**
A website Google cannot find is not a business asset. SEO needs to be built in from day one, not bolted on later.
[See our local SEO citation building service](/local-seo-citation-building)

**Writing copy without a brand voice**
Homepage copy that says everything connects with no one. Every word needs to speak to one audience with one clear message.

**Going live without testing**
Broken links, slow load speeds, and mobile display issues damage the first impression your brand makes.

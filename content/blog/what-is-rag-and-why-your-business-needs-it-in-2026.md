## Introduction

Many businesses want AI.

But most businesses do not actually need an AI that simply generates text.

They need an AI system that understands:

* their products
* their services
* their documents
* their customers
* their internal knowledge

Traditional AI systems sometimes create answers that sound correct but may contain inaccurate information.

This becomes risky for:

* customer support
* healthcare
* education
* SaaS platforms
* legal systems
* internal business workflows

That is where RAG enters.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation allows AI to retrieve information from your own business knowledge before creating responses.

Instead of guessing, AI becomes more informed.

---

## What Is RAG?

RAG stands for:

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation

It combines two major processes:

### Retrieval

The system searches relevant information from:

* PDFs
* databases
* websites
* knowledge bases
* internal documents
* FAQs
* product data

### Generation

The AI model then uses retrieved information to generate an answer.

Instead of depending entirely on pre-trained knowledge, AI receives real business context first.

---

## How RAG Works

### Step 1 — Business Data Collection

Information sources may include:

* websites
* support documentation
* CRM systems
* FAQs
* PDFs
* databases
* APIs

---

### Step 2 — Data Processing

The system converts information into searchable formats.

Often this includes:

* embeddings
* vector databases
* indexing systems

---

### Step 3 — Information Retrieval

When users ask questions:

The AI retrieves the most relevant information.

---

### Step 4 — Response Generation

The AI combines retrieved information with language understanding and creates contextual responses.

# What Is a Fintech App?

A fintech app is a financial technology application designed to improve or automate financial services.

These apps help users:
- manage money  
- transfer payments  
- invest  
- track expenses  
- access banking services  
- automate financial tasks  

Fintech apps are used by:
- startups  
- banks  
- payment companies  
- investors  
- freelancers  
- e-commerce businesses

The best fintech startups solve very specific problems.

- easier freelancer payments  
- faster business invoicing  
- better budgeting tools  
- digital banking access

# Step 2: Research Financial Regulations

Fintech apps often require compliance with:
- payment regulations  
- financial laws  
- security standards  

Ignoring regulations early creates major problems later.

---

# Step 3: Design Trust-Focused UX

Fintech UI should feel:
- stable  
- clear  
- reliable  

Users hesitate when financial apps feel confusing.

# Step 4: Choose the Right Technology Stack

Popular fintech technologies include:

- React Native

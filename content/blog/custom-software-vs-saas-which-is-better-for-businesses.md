# What Is SaaS?

SaaS stands for:
**Software as a Service**

These are cloud-based platforms businesses subscribe to monthly or yearly.

Examples include:
- Shopify  
- Slack  
- Trello  
- HubSpot  
- Notion  
- Salesforce  

SaaS products are designed for mass usage.

They provide standardized solutions for many businesses.

---

# What Is Custom Software?

Custom software is built specifically for a business’s unique workflows and operational needs.

Instead of adapting your business around generic software…

the software adapts around your business.

Custom systems may include:
- CRM platforms  
- automation software  
- inventory systems  
- healthcare dashboards  
- logistics systems  
- enterprise applications  
- SaaS platforms  
- AI-powered workflows

As operations grow, software becomes business infrastructure.

That’s why choosing the right software model matters.

## What Is Web Development Cost?

Web development cost refers to the total investment required to design, develop, deploy, and maintain a website or web application.

Think of building a website like constructing a commercial building.

A small shop costs less than a shopping mall.

Similarly:

- A landing page costs less than an eCommerce platform.
- A corporate website costs less than a SaaS application.
- A basic website costs less than an AI-powered business platform.

The final price depends on:

- Features
- Design complexity
- Technology stack
- SEO requirements
- Integrations
- Security
- Scalability
- Performance optimization

Modern frameworks like React and Next.js have changed how businesses approach development because they provide exceptional performance, flexibility, and long-term scalability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a website cost in Pakistan in 2026?

A professional website may cost between PKR 63,000 and PKR 2,500,000+, depending on complexity, features, and business requirements.

### Why are React websites more expensive?

React applications require specialized expertise but provide better scalability, maintainability, and performance.

### Is Next.js worth the investment?

Yes. Businesses often choose Next.js because of its SEO advantages, faster performance, and improved user experience.

### How much does an eCommerce website cost?

Custom eCommerce development typically ranges from PKR 150,000 to PKR 1,100,000+.

### What is the cost of a SaaS MVP?

Most SaaS MVP projects start around PKR 1,100,000 and can exceed PKR 2,500,000 depending on functionality.

### Does SEO affect development cost?

Yes. Proper SEO architecture, schema implementation, Core Web Vitals optimization, and technical SEO increase development effort but provide long-term benefits.
